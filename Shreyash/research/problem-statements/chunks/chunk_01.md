It should also identify the key contributing factors responsible for the predicted delay and provide actionable recommendations for mitigating those risks. Interactive dashboards should enable policymakers and administrators to monitor high-risk projects, visualize delay trends across districts and states, and prioritize interventions based on predictive insights. The solution should support continuous learning by updating prediction models as new project data becomes available, thereby improving prediction accuracy over time.
